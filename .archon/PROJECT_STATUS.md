### Known Blockers (do not retry)

- Direct Brownian QV is not the generic QV comparison theorem. `ProbabilityTheory.quadraticVariation_brownian` now follows directly from `brownianQuadraticVariation = brownianDeterministicTime`; do not claim this proves `quadraticVariation isLocalMartingale_brownian isCadlag_brownian` agrees with time. The generic choice-based comparison still requires the square-norm local-submartingale, Doob-Meyer, and predictable finite-variation uniqueness debts.
- `ProbabilityTheory.brownianQuadraticVariation_normalized_decomposition`: the proof body is closed, but `lean_verify` reports transitive `sorryAx` through the existing deterministic-time integrable-sup dependency `ProbabilityTheory.hasIntegrableSup_brownianDeterministicTime`.
